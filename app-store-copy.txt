DESCRIPTION  (4000 chars max — Apple shows ~250 chars before "More")
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SonoPilot turns any smartphone into an intelligent ultrasound assistant.
Point your camera at any ultrasound screen — AI instantly labels anatomy,
calculates measurements, and flags key findings in seconds.

── HOW IT WORKS ──
1. Snap a photo of any ultrasound screen (no special equipment needed)
2. AI analyzes the image — structures labeled, measurements calculated,
   anomalies automatically flagged
3. Review annotated findings, share results, or export a one-tap PDF report

── 31+ PROTOCOLS SUPPORTED ──
• Emergency / eFAST (FAST, extended FAST)
• Cardiac Echo (parasternal, apical, subcostal views)
• OB/GYN (first trimester, biophysical profile)
• Vascular (DVT, aorta, IVC)
• Musculoskeletal (MSK)
• Lung / Pleural
• Renal & Bladder
• Soft Tissue
• And more

── KEY FEATURES ──
• Instant AI structure labeling
• Automated measurements
• Anomaly detection and flagging
• AI chat — ask questions about your scan
• One-tap PDF report export
• Automatic patient data redaction (PHI protection)
• HIPAA-ready privacy architecture
• Works offline — view past scans without connectivity

── BUILT FOR CLINICIANS ──
Designed for emergency medicine physicians, critical care specialists,
hospitalists, residents, medical students, and point-of-care ultrasound
(POCUS) practitioners.

IMPORTANT: SonoPilot is an educational and clinical decision support tool.
It is NOT FDA-cleared and is not intended for primary diagnosis. Always
apply clinical judgment and follow your institution's protocols.

── PLANS ──
Free: 5 AI analyses per month, all 31 protocols
Pro: Unlimited analyses — $9.99/month or $69.99/year
